TEMPLATE #2 — CASH FLOW MODELING

USE THIS to avoid running out of money.

PROMPT:
"Build a 12-month cash flow forecast for: [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]

Include:

1) Operating cash flow (collections, payments)
2) Investment cash flow (capital purchases, asset sales)
3) Financing cash flow (loans, equity, dividends)
4) Beginning cash, ending cash (monthly)
5) Cumulative cash position
6) Cash runway (months until cash runs out)
7) Break-even analysis

Assumptions:
- Revenue growth: [X% monthly]
- Collection time: [days]
- Payment terms: [days]
- Fixed costs: [$/month]

Provide:
- Month-by-month cash flow table
- Burn rate calculation
- Runway months
- What-if scenarios (slower growth, longer payments)"

ARVEN TIP: Profitability ≠ cash. You can be profitable and still run out of cash.

USE THIS to track what matters.

PROMPT:
"Define key financial metrics for: [DESCRIBE BUSINESS]

Calculate:

1) Revenue metrics (MRR, ARR, growth %)
2) Customer metrics (CAC, LTV, payback period)
3) Profitability (gross margin, net margin, EBITDA)
4) Efficiency (revenue per employee, sales efficiency)
5) Liquidity (current ratio, quick ratio)
6) Leverage (debt/equity, interest coverage)
7) Return (ROE, ROIC, ROAS)

- Formula and calculation

- Target ranges
- Dashboard for tracking"

ARVEN TIP: You need 5-7 key metrics max. Too many = noise.

2) Actual vs Prior Year
3) Forecast vs Actual

Calculate:
1) Dollar variance (actual - budget)
2) Percentage variance
3) Root causes (volume, price, efficiency, mix)
4) Impact assessment (major variances only)
5) Corrective actions

Period: [Month/Quarter]

Provide:
- Variance summary table
- Detailed analysis of variances >5%

ARVEN TIP: Always investigate variances >10%. They reveal what's really happening.

TEMPLATE #5 — BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

USE THIS to know when you'll make money.

PROMPT:
"Calculate break-even point for: [DESCRIBE PRODUCT/SERVICE]

Include:

1) Fixed costs (salaries, rent, insurance, etc.)
2) Variable costs per unit (COGS, commission, shipping)
3) Selling price per unit
4) Contribution margin (price - variable cost)
5) Break-even volume (units to sell)
6) Break-even revenue ($ to generate)
7) Break-even timeline (months)
8) Margin of safety (how much volume cushion)

Provide:
- Break-even calculation
- Sensitivity analysis (what if price changes 10%?)
- Chart showing profit/loss at different volumes
- Timeline to break-even"

ARVEN TIP: Know your break-even before launching. Don't build until you can.
